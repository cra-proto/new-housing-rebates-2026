The Ontario enhanced new housing rebate (ENHR) is a temporary rebate that provides a top-up to the Ontario new housing rebate. It applies to homes valued up to $1.85 million that are purchased from a builder between April 1, 2026, and March 31, 2027, and owner-built homes where construction begins during the same period.

How much can you get

The Ontario ENHR, combined with the Ontario new housing rebate, provides:

Rebate amount
New home value Rebate amount
Up to $1 million Full rebate (100% of the 8% provincial part of the HST paid, up to $80,000)
 Above $1 million, up to $1.5 million  Flat rebate of $80,000
Above $1.5 million and below $1.85 million Partial rebate of the provincial part of HST paid 
$1.85 million and above   Only the Ontario new housing rebate of up to $24,000 is available

Ontario new home affordability payment (ONHAP)

If you are eligible for the Ontario ENHR, you may also be eligible for the ONHAP.

The ONHAP provides an amount up to $50,000, equivalent to up to the 5% federal part of the HST. This amount would be paid directly by the Province of Ontario.

The ONHAP is reduced by any GST/HST new housing rebate or first-time home buyers’ (FTHB) GST/HST rebate that you may be entitled to.

Interaction with the FTHB rebates

If you are eligible for the FTHB GST/HST rebate for the 5% federal part of the HST, you must claim that rebate first to determine your eligibility for the ONHAP. If there is any amount related to the 5% federal part of the HST that could not be rebated through the FTHB GST/HST rebate, you may be able to recover that amount through the ONHAP.

If you are eligible for both the Ontario ENHR and the Ontario FTHB rebate for the 8% provincial part of the HST, you can choose to claim either or both of these rebates. The total amount of all rebates for the 8% provincial part of the HST cannot exceed the lesser of:

  • $80,000
  • The provincial part of the HST payable for the purchase or construction of the home

This means that the rebate cannot exceed the tax paid or payable, including any amount paid or credited by the builder.

Who can apply

You may be eligible for an Ontario ENHR rebate if:

  1. You are eligible for a GST/HST new housing rebate

    Check eligibility for the GST/HST new housing rebate

  2. You live in Ontario

  3. You meet the applicable key dates and purchase price or fair market value (FMV) maximums

    If you bought a home from a builder or a share in a co-op

    Applicable key dates

    You must meet all of the following conditions:

    • You bought the home between April 1, 2026, and March 31, 2027
    • Construction begins by December 31, 2028
    • Construction is substantially completed by December 31, 2031
    • Possession or ownership is transferred to you by December 31, 2032

    Purchase price maximums

    The purchase price is less than:

    If you bought the land
    $1,850,000
    If you lease the land
    $2,090,500
    If you built your own home

    Applicable key dates

    You must meet all of the following conditions:

    • Construction begins between April 1, 2026, and March 31, 2027
    • Construction is substantially completed by December 31, 2029

    FMV maximums

    The FMV is less than:

    $1,850,000

Restrictions for the Ontario ENHR

Eligibility for this rebate is limited by the following restrictions:

Assignment sales

For assignment sales, to be eligible for the Ontario ENHR, both the initial agreement of purchase and sale with the builder and the assignment agreement must be entered into between April 1, 2026, and March 31, 2027.

Change in agreement

If you entered into an agreement with a builder before April 1, 2026, and you make changes to the agreement such that it is considered to be entered into on or after April 1, 2026, for the purpose of eligibility for the Ontario ENHR, the agreement is deemed to have been entered into before April 1, 2026, and you are not entitled to the rebate. In this case you may still be eligible for the Ontario new housing rebate or Ontario first-time home buyers’ rebate.

New agreement

If you entered into an agreement with a builder before April 1, 2026, the agreement is terminated and another agreement is entered into on or after April 1, 2026, to be eligible for the Ontario ENHR, the new agreement must have been entered into for bona fide purposes other than to obtain the Ontario ENHR.

How to apply

You will apply for this rebate together with any (?) other housing rebate at the same time.
